These are original synthetic reference voices generated for Zyrah, not recordings of a real speaker. They were designed with OmniVoice Q4_K_M, seed 41 (A) and 73 (B), 32 steps, and the design instructions in server/neural-tts.js. No VTuber reference was used. A profile name is not a human quality approval.

The WAV and matching UTF-8 text are authoritative identity anchors. RVQ files were encoded with the matching OmniVoice Q4 tokenizer; do not reuse them with another tokenizer. Keep profile IDs/versioned assets together. Do not silently regenerate an installed profile.
