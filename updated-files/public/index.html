A note about privacy
Chats and notes are stored in a local SQLite database, without encryption. Cloud models receive recent chat and selected notes. Cloud voice sends your recorded speech for transcription; browser dictation may use your browser vendor’s servers. Raw microphone recordings are not saved by this app. This is a single-user local application.
